Quantum Code Generation

Welcome! This tool is designed to bridge the gap between classical software engineering and the quantum realm. It empowers researchers, students, and developers to intuitively design, simulate, and execute quantum circuits with unprecedented ease and efficiency.

Research group

Developed by the Alarcos Research Group from the University of Castilla-La Mancha (UCLM). Alarcos focuses on transforming software quality, security, and the future of quantum software engineering.

Root system: QSauron

This code generation tool is a vital component of QSauron, a comprehensive lifecycle and management system for quantum software. QSauron integrates multiple modular tools to provide a seamless quantum development experience from start to finish.
